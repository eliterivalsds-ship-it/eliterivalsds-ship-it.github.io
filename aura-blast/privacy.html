Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 9, 2026

Welcome to Aura Blast, developed by Jiro World Studio (Hyderabad, Telangana, India). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights when you download and play our game on Android.

By installing or playing Aura Blast, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum information necessary to run the game and its features:

  • Google Play Games Services (GPGS) Identity: When you optionally sign in, we use GPGS to authenticate you. We receive your GPGS Player ID and profile name to identify your account and enable cloud saves. Your total star count is also submitted to the Play Games leaderboard, where it appears with your Play Games name and rank, governed by Google's policies.
  • Game Progression Data: Your level progress, scores, in-game currency, and inventory are stored locally on your device. If you are signed into GPGS, this data is backed up to Google's servers (Saved Games/Snapshots) so you can restore it across devices. This data is not stored on our own private servers.
  • Device and Usage Data: Aura Blast keeps simple diagnostic counters on your device only. We do not operate our own analytics or crash-reporting servers. Google Play (Android vitals) may give us aggregated crash and performance statistics. This data does not identify you personally.
  • Advertising Data: To show ads, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and general device information (see Section 3).
  • Purchase Records: If you make an in-app purchase, Google Play Billing processes and records the transaction. We receive confirmation of entitlements but do not store your payment card details.
  • Notifications: If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders (for example, daily rewards) locally on your device. No notification data is sent to us.
  • Game Announcements: The game occasionally downloads small public files (announcements and the current version number) from a static web host. These requests contain no account or personal information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account via Google Play Games Services.
  • To back up and restore your game progression locally on your device and via GPGS Cloud Saves.
  • To process and fulfil in-app purchases and restore entitlements across reinstalls.
  • To show banner ads and optional Rewarded Video Ads, and credit in-game rewards upon completion.
  • To improve the game using aggregated, non-personal performance statistics.

3. Third-Party Services

Aura Blast integrates the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Games Services (GPGS) — Used for authentication and cloud save backups. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob — Banner ads and optional Rewarded Video Ads. AdMob may collect your IP address and mobile advertising ID to serve and measure ads, which may be personalised. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and you can decline. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing — Payment processing for in-app purchases. Google Privacy Policy

The optional Remove Ads purchase removes banner ads.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third parties or data brokers. Your data is shared only with the infrastructure providers listed above, strictly to the extent required to operate the game's features.

5. Children's Privacy

Aura Blast is a family-friendly match-3 game, but it is not directed at children under 13 and is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads shown in the game are limited to a general-audience content rating.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has used the game or provided personal information, please contact us at jiroworld05@gmail.com and we will delete it.

6. Data Deletion Request

Since Aura Blast primarily stores your progression data locally on your device or linked to your Google Account:

  • Game Data: You can delete your local game data by uninstalling the app. You can delete your cloud saves via your Google Account settings.
  • Leaderboard Scores: These are held by Google Play Games Services and can be reset or removed through Google.
  • Advertising ID: You can reset your advertising ID in your Android device settings.

Aura Blast does not keep an account database of its own. If you would like us to confirm that we hold no data about you, or to help with any deletion request, email jiroworld05@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the game after changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
